Pulse - messaging automation
============================

The version number is in VERSION.txt and in the dashboard under Help.

START
  Double-click Run-App.bat (or whatsapp-auto.exe). The dashboard opens in its own window.
  Run-Server.bat starts Pulse without a window; open http://127.0.0.1:8085 in your browser.

FIRST SIGN-IN
  Pulse creates a one-time password the first time it starts. It is shown in the black
  console window and saved in FIRST-RUN-PASSWORD.txt inside the data folder
  (%APPDATA%\Pulse). After signing in you choose your own password and the file is deleted.
  There is no built-in master password.

FORGOT THE PASSWORD?
  Close Pulse, right-click Reset-Password.bat and choose "Run as administrator".
  You get a new one-time password. Messages, contacts and the WhatsApp connection are kept.
  The dashboard shows a notice for 7 days.

CRITICAL: PHONE NUMBER & SETUP REQUIREMENTS BEFORE YOU START
  Pulse connects through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).
  Unlike legacy tools or consumer WhatsApp, Meta's Cloud API has two vital requirements:

  1. DEDICATED PHONE NUMBER (CANNOT BE USED ON MOBILE AT THE SAME TIME):
     Meta does NOT permit a number registered to the Cloud API to be active in the
     WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business mobile app on your smartphone at the same time.
     - Strongly recommended: Use a separate business SIM, a virtual number, or a landline.
     - If you register an existing phone number, you MUST first delete/deregister the
       WhatsApp account on your mobile phone. Once registered to Cloud API, all chats,
       replies, and message history are managed exclusively through the Pulse Web Inbox.
     - Attempting to log back into WhatsApp on your phone will disconnect the Cloud API.

  2. META PREREQUISITES ("HEAVY SETUP"):
     To connect Pulse, you need:
     - A Meta Developer Account (developers.facebook.com) and Meta Business Portfolio.
     - A permanent System User Access Token with whatsapp_business_messaging permissions.
     - A fixed HTTPS tunnel (e.g., a Named Cloudflare Tunnel or static ngrok domain) to
       receive incoming customer messages (Meta delivers incoming messages via webhooks).
     - Approved message templates in WhatsApp Manager for outbound broadcasts (outside 24 hrs).
     Every step is documented in detail in docs\meta-whatsapp-setup.md and SETUP-GUIDE.md.

CONNECT WHATSAPP
  From developers.facebook.com, you need: the phone number ID, the WhatsApp Business account ID,
  a permanent access token (Business settings > System users), and the app secret
  (App settings > Basic).
  Incoming messages need a fixed https address or tunnel (e.g. a named Cloudflare
  tunnel with a fixed domain, a static ngrok domain, or a reverse proxy like Caddy).
  Quick tunnels (cloudflared tunnel --url...) get a new address on every start, which
  breaks Meta's webhook; use a fixed address for daily business.
  Privacy note: managed tunnels (Cloudflare, ngrok) terminate TLS at their edge;
  for 100% private deployments, terminate TLS directly on your own infrastructure.
  Paste everything in Settings > WhatsApp connection, press Test connection and Save, then
  in Meta's WhatsApp > Configuration set the Callback URL and Verify token shown by Pulse
  and subscribe to "messages". Only the webhook address works through the tunnel.

THEN
  Follow the "Get started" list on the Home page: read WhatsApp's messaging rules,
  connect the Business API, receive a first message, enter your business details,
  send a test to your own mobile, switch automatic replies on.

24 HOURS AND TEMPLATES
  WhatsApp lets you reply freely for 24 hours after a customer writes. After that only
  message templates approved by Meta (WhatsApp Manager) can be sent. Enter them under
  "Official WhatsApp template" in campaigns and scheduled messages. Meta bills some
  messages, mostly templates.

TRY YOUR REPLIES
  Auto-replies > Test a message shows the reply a customer would get, without sending.
  Practice mode (Settings) shows what would be sent without sending anything.

YOUR DATA
  Contacts, messages and settings data are stored encrypted in %APPDATA%\Pulse, readable only
  by your Windows account. The access token and app secret are stored sealed with your
  Windows account. An encrypted backup is saved every day (last 7 kept); see
  Settings > Privacy and data. An extra backup copy can go to a folder on this computer
  (for example a USB drive or a synced folder); network folders are not allowed.
  "Sign out" only signs you out and deletes nothing. To delete all data, use
  Settings > Privacy and data; Pulse asks for your password first. Close Pulse normally (Ctrl+C in its window, or
  scripts\Stop-Background.ps1) so the last changes are saved.

WINDOWS BLOCKS THE PROGRAM?
  Unless this copy was signed with a trusted code-signing certificate, Windows may stop it:
  - SmartScreen ("Windows protected your PC"): choose "More info" > "Run anyway", but only if
    the checksum in SHA256SUMS.txt matches (PowerShell: Get-FileHash whatsapp-auto.exe).
    Run Unblock-Pulse.bat to remove the internet download flag.
  - Smart App Control blocks unsigned programs completely and cannot be bypassed for one
    program on Windows 11. Use an official signed release or trust your organization's
    certificate (BUILD.md).
  To check a copy: right-click whatsapp-auto.exe > Properties > Digital Signatures.

UPGRADING
  Close the old version first. From 1.x, contacts, messages and settings are kept; connect
  the Business API as above. The old WhatsApp Web session files are no longer used:
  Settings > Privacy and data offers to delete them. Remove Pulse from the phone's
  Linked devices list. Pulse imports contacts and messages from versions before 1.4 into
  the encrypted data folder and keeps the unencrypted originals in
  %APPDATA%\Pulse\old-version-backup until you delete them.
